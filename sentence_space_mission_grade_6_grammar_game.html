📚 Quick Grammar Cheat Sheet

1. Simple Sentence

Has one independent clause (one complete idea with a subject and a verb). Can have compound subjects or verbs, but still one main thought.

Example: Sarah and Maya played soccer in the park.

2. Compound Sentence

Joins two complete sentences using a comma + FANBOYS:

For And Nor But Or Yet So

Example: I wanted to go swimming, but the pool was closed.

3. Complex Sentence

Combines 1 independent clause + 1 dependent clause using a subordinating conjunction:

because when although if since while before / after

Example: Although it was cold, we ate ice cream.
